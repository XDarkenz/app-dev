# Favorite Series (In Order)

## NUMBER 4: Jojo's Bizzare Adventure
> Coolest anime with the weirdest plot and fighting system involving stands and stand users with unique sets of skills

## NUMBER 3: Jujutsu Kaisen
> Another cool anime that focuses on Japanese mythology involving curses and the art of sorcery fighting in order to exorcise curses, although the anime focuses on ideologies of individualism and the strengths that come with egocentrism while also exploring the sacrifice it demands.

## NUMBER 2: Better Call Saul
> An American TV Series spin-off of the American TV Series Breaking Bad, it is centered on a lawyer namedd Jimmy MCGill as he struggles in his career, he uses his niche skills as being a con artist to further his career in the Law but it comes with a price.

## NUMBER 1: Breaking Bad
> An American TV Series centered on a timid chemistry teacher who is suddenly diagnosed with lung cancer. Determined to not leave his family alone with no financial support, he uses his knowledge in chemistry to become a drug manufacturer to get as much money as possible in the illegal drug business in the shortest amount of time while his clock is ticking. But as the show progresses, the events that transpire within Walter's predicaments starts to show a side of him he never knew he had, a side brimming to show itself and fulfill its ambitions and pride.
